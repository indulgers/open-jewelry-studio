# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Confirmed: Vite, React, TypeScript, Three.js, React Three Fiber and drei; no proprietary jewelry SDK.

## Users

A full-stack developer and modeling/product colleagues preparing a commercial DIY jewelry configurator.

## Product Purpose

Evaluate interactive jewelry against commercial display requirements: readable metal and stone detail, accurate product geometry and traceable assets. The current app is a rendering sample; production SKU correspondence and commerce systems remain future work.

## Capabilities and Constraints

An MIT sample ring with the user's round gemstone, a library of 19 supplied gemstone shapes, two procedural shank styles, three metal appearances, two lighting presets, camera controls, local saving, quality modes and four-view image export. No price, payment, inventory or AR. Source gemstones have no declared physical units; the sample ring is not a production SKU. Small mobile viewport verification is not physical device testing. External asset licensing and attribution are required.

## Evidence on Hand

Approved implementation plan in ../docs/superpowers/. The supplied 3DM gemstones have been converted and checked; provenance is recorded in assets/atelier-gems-provenance.json. Blue Nile live-viewer comparison and commercial limitations are documented in docs/commercial-render-review.md. No fabricated product, visual-parity or performance claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Named keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus, reduced-motion preference, understandable loading and recovery states.
