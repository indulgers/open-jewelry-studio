# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Confirmed: Vite, React, TypeScript, Three.js, React Three Fiber and drei; no proprietary jewelry SDK.

## Users

A full-stack developer evaluating an open-source jewelry configurator with prospective product and modeling colleagues.

## Product Purpose

Demonstrate a real interactive ring, with procedural shanks and an externally sourced, licensed gemstone GLB. Evaluate rendering and interaction before connecting commercial systems.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Two shank styles, three metal appearances, three visual gemstone sizes, camera controls, local saving and quality modes. No price, payment, inventory, production CAD or AR. Small mobile viewport verification is not physical device testing. External asset licensing and attribution are required.

## Evidence on Hand

Approved design and implementation plan in ../docs/superpowers/. Real gemstone file still to be acquired and checked; no fabricated product or performance claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Named keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus, reduced-motion preference, understandable loading and recovery states.
